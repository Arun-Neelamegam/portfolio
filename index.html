Dashboard 01 — Hospitality Analytics

Hospitality Performance Dashboard

A unified real-time view of hospitality performance across properties and markets. Tracks Revenue, RevPAR, ADR, and Occupancy % to identify revenue gaps, demand trends, and underperforming properties. Interactive analysis by room category, city, and booking platform supports data-driven pricing and channel strategy decisions.

Dashboard 02 — Sales KPI

Sales Performance vs. Targets Dashboard

Built on a star-schema model with advanced DAX measures: Total Sales, YTD Sales, Variance %, and Target Achievement using CALCULATE, DIVIDE, DATESYTD, and context transition techniques. Features week-over-week comparisons, conditional formatting, dynamic KPIs, and cross-filtering by team, salesperson, and time period.
